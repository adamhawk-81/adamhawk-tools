CONVERSION TOOLS / WIRE GAUGE

Wire Gauge Converter

Convert nominal solid-wire diameter between gauge designations, inches, and millimeters.

How the Calculation Works

1. Select the wire gauge system

American Wire Gauge (AWG), British Standard Wire Gauge (SWG), and US Steel Wire Gauge (Washburn & Moen) use different diameter tables. The gauge system—not simply the wire material—determines the nominal diameter.

2. Convert the diameter

For AWG, nominal diameter in inches follows the defined geometric progression:

Diameter (in) = 0.005 × 92(36 − gauge) / 39

For SWG and US Steel Wire Gauge, the converter uses nominal reference tables. Millimeters are obtained by multiplying inches by 25.4.

Example: 16 AWG solid wire has a nominal diameter of approximately 0.05082 inches (1.291 mm).

3. Find the nearest gauge

Enter a diameter to find the nearest nominal gauge in the selected system.

This tool does not calculate insulation thickness, stranded-wire outside diameter, or electrical ampacity.

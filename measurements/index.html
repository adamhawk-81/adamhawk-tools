Length & Measurement Converter

Convert instantly between metric and imperial length measurements, including fractional inches.

How the Calculation Works

1. Enter a measurement

Enter a value in any unit field. The converter uses millimeters as its internal common unit, then updates the remaining fields.

2. Apply exact unit relationships

1 inch = 25.4 mm   ·   1 foot = 12 inches
1 cm = 10 mm   ·   1 meter = 1000 mm

Example: 1½ inches = 38.1 mm = 3.81 cm = 0.0381 m.

3. Round fractional inches

The fractional display rounds the calculated decimal-inch value to the selected fraction denominator. Other unit fields retain their independent decimal conversions.

Fractional values are approximations at the selected precision. The decimal unit conversions use exact conversion factors.

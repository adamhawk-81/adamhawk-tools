Sheet Metal Gauge Converter

Convert nominal sheet metal gauge thickness to inches and millimeters using the appropriate material standard.

How the Calculation Works

1. Select the material standard

Sheet metal gauge is a nominal thickness designation. The same gauge number can represent different thicknesses for standard steel, galvanized steel, stainless steel, and aluminum.

2. Convert thickness

After looking up the nominal thickness in inches, convert it to millimeters using the exact inch definition.

Millimeters = Inches × 25.4

Example: 16 gauge standard sheet steel is nominally 0.0598 inches, or approximately 1.519 mm.

3. Reverse lookup

Entering an inch or millimeter thickness selects the nearest nominal gauge in the chosen material table. The entered measurement is not rounded to that gauge.

Gauge tables are reference values. Verify the actual thickness and tolerances with the supplier.

FORGING CALCULATORS / SCROLL GEOMETRY

Tapered Scroll Calculator

Design a square-section tapered scroll and estimate the original square stock length needed to forge it. All dimensions are in inches.

How the Calculation Works

A tapered scroll begins as square stock, is drawn into a progressively smaller square section, and then bent into a spiral. The calculator uses spiral geometry and conservation of volume to estimate the required material.

1. Calculate the spiral geometry

The centerline follows an Archimedean spiral, where radius increases uniformly with angle:

r(θ) = r₀ + pθ / (2π)

r₀ = inner centerline radius
p = radial pitch per turn
θ = angle in radians

The calculator divides the spiral into many short segments and adds their lengths to estimate the developed centerline length.

2. Calculate the tapered bar volume

For a square bar tapering linearly from starting width W to finished tip width T:

V = L × (W² + WT + T²) / 3

V = finished taper volume
L = developed centerline length
W = starting square width
T = finished square tip width

3. Calculate the original stock length

Divide the finished volume by the starting cross-sectional area:

Lstock = V / W²

Example:

A 12" long taper drawn from 1" square stock to a ¼" square tip:

V = 12 × (1² + 1 × 0.25 + 0.25²) / 3

V = 5.250 in³

Lstock = 5.250 / 1²

Original stock required = 5.250"

Additional reference: diameter and allowance

4. Calculate radial pitch from outside diameter

p = (D/2 − r₀ − Wouter/2) / N

D = target outside diameter
Wouter = width at the outer end
N = number of turns

5. Add material allowance

Lprepare = Lstock × (1 + P/100)

P is the additional material allowance percentage. A theoretical 5.250" requirement with 5% allowance becomes approximately 5.513".

This is a geometric estimating tool, not a physical forging or bending simulation. Actual results may vary due to distortion, bending strain, scale loss, and forging technique.
